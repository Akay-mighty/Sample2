FAQ

Questions? We have answers

Everything you need to know about banking with FLAMZY.

Is FLAMZY a real bank?
FLAMZY operates as a digital banking service backed by a licensed partner bank. Your deposits are NDIC-insured up to ₦500,000, and we comply with all CBN regulations to keep your money safe and accessible.
How long does it take to open an account?
Opening a FLAMZY account takes less than 3 minutes. Download the app, provide your BVN and a valid ID, complete the KYC verification, and you're ready to start banking immediately.
Are there hidden fees?
No hidden fees, ever. We charge zero maintenance fees, zero card issuance fees, and free transfers up to ₦10,000 daily. Transfers above that incur a flat ₦10 fee per transaction.
What is the interest rate on savings?
FLAMZY offers up to 15% APY on locked savings and 8% APY on flexible savings. Interest is calculated daily and paid monthly. Choose from Fixed, Target, or Flex savings to match your goals.
How do I get a FLAMZY Visa card?
Your virtual Visa card is generated instantly and free upon account creation. The physical card can be requested from the app and is delivered to your address within 3–5 business days.
What if I lose my phone?
Your account is protected by biometric authentication and 2FA, so no one can access it without your fingerprint or face. You can also freeze your account instantly from any device by contacting our 24/7 support.

How FLAMZY transfers work

  • Transfers between FLAMZY users are instant and free.
  • Recipients must have a FLAMZY account — look them up by email or account number (FLZ-XXXXXXXX).
  • Sender's balance is debited atomically using Firebase transactions — no double-spending.
  • If the recipient is offline, the credit queues and posts automatically on their next login.
