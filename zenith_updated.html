About Us

Your Trusted Source for
Premium Electrical
& Lighting

Zenith Electrical & Lighting is a premier importer and bulk distributor of commercial, industrial, and decorative lighting solutions, headquartered in Alaba International Market — Lagos.

We bridge the gap between heavy-duty electrical functionality and contemporary interior styling, serving contractors, developers, and retail merchants across West Africa.

Heavy-duty LED systems engineered for factories, warehouses, and commercial properties
Exquisite chandeliers and pendant arrays for premium residences and hotels
Competitive bulk wholesale rates direct from Africa's largest electronics hub
Reliable distribution networks across all 36 states of Nigeria
