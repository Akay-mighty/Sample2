Our Process

From Brief to Beautiful Product

A clear, streamlined process — from first contact to delivery at your door.

01
Consultation & Brief
Share your brand assets, quantities, and specifications via WhatsApp or email. We advise on the best materials and formats for your budget.
02
Design & Proof
Our design team creates a detailed digital mockup for your approval. No production begins until you're fully satisfied with the design proof.
03
Factory Production
Your order runs at our dedicated Ilupeju manufacturing facility with quality control checkpoints at every stage of the process.
04
Packaged & Delivered
Orders are carefully packaged and dispatched. Lagos orders are hand-delivered; nationwide orders ship via trusted logistics partners.
